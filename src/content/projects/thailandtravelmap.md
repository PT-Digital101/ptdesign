## Overview

**ThailandTravelMap.com**—“Uncover Thailand with Netflix”—is an immersive digital travel platform co-created with Netflix, the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT), the Department of National Parks (DNP), and the Creative Economy Agency (CEA). It invites both local and international visitors to explore Thailand through Netflix-set destinations, delivering curated content that spans nature, gastronomy, culture, and heritage.

## Objectives

Introduce users to lesser-known Thai destinations featured in Netflix productions.

Offer a visually compelling and user-friendly guide aligned with Netflix’s storytelling.

Inspire authentic travel experiences by combining insider knowledge, multimedia, and interactive maps.

## My Roles ((UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

Designed a clean, intuitive interface that aligns with both Netflix branding and Thailand’s cultural richness.

Built interactive destinations galleries across **four main categories**:

1. **Nature** – From waterfalls and national parks to misty viewpoints

2. **Gastronomy** – Street food, fine dining, and culinary secrets

3. **Culture** – Festivals, communities, and unique local experiences

4.**Heritage** – Temples, historical monuments, and traditional landmarks

Integrated a visually rich **interactive map** to help users explore handpicked points of interest by region.

Deployed WordPress for flexible content updates, with clear content structure for multimedia stories.

## Results

Delivered a modern, media-infused travel platform that stands out for storytelling and interactivity.

Enabled broad appeal—gracefully combining entertainment from Netflix with real travel inspiration.

Built a scalable, content-driven WordPress foundation for ongoing updates.
