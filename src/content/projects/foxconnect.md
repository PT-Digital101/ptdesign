## Overview

FoxConnect is a comprehensive booking and CRM platform tailored for service-oriented businesses in Thailand. By integrating seamlessly with LINE Messenger—the country’s most widely used messaging app—it enables businesses to manage appointments, customer relationships, and communications efficiently, all within a familiar interface.

## Objectives

1. **User-Centric Design:** Develop an intuitive and accessible interface that caters to both business owners and customers.

2. **Seamless Integration:** Ensure smooth synchronization between the platform and LINE Messenger for real-time communication and booking.

3. **Scalability:** Design a system capable of supporting various business models, from single-location salons to multi-branch clinics.

4. **Efficiency:** Implement features that streamline operations, reduce manual tasks, and enhance customer satisfaction.

## My Role (UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

As the lead UX/UI designer and web developer, I was responsible for:

1. **User Research:** Conducting interviews and surveys with business owners and customers to identify pain points and needs.

2. **Interface Design:** Designing a clean, modern aesthetic that aligns with the brand and enhances usability.

3. **Development:** Implementing the design into a fully functional website, ensuring responsiveness and performance across devices

## Results

**Enhanced User Experience:** Simplified workflows and intuitive design reduced booking errors and improved customer satisfaction.

**Operational Efficiency:** Automation of scheduling and communication tasks freed up time for business owners to focus on service delivery.

**Scalability:** The platform’s flexible architecture supported the growth of businesses, accommodating multiple branches and practitioners.

**Market Adoption:** Increased adoption among service businesses in Thailand, positioning FoxConnect as a leading solution in the industry.
