## Overview

Inception Idea is a Bangkok-based interior design firm specializing in creating functional and aesthetically pleasing spaces. Their portfolio includes residential homes, wellness centers, retail shops, and more. The company emphasizes a personalized approach, ensuring each project reflects the client’s unique style and needs.

## Objectives

1. Develop a user-friendly website that showcases Inception Idea’s diverse portfolio and services.

2. Highlight key projects to demonstrate the company’s expertise and versatility.

3. Provide clear information about services offered, including architectural design, interior design, shop design, wellness spaces, and residential design.

4. Enhance online visibility through SEO best practices and engaging content.

## My Role (UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

As the UX/UI Designer and WordPress Developer, I led the project through several key phases:

1. **UX Strategy:** Designed an intuitive layout that guides users through services, projects, and contact information.

2. **Visual Design:** Created a clean, modern aesthetic that aligns with Inception Idea’s branding and values.

3. **WordPress Development:** Built a responsive website using WordPress, ensuring ease of content management and scalability.

4. **SEO Optimization:** Implemented SEO best practices to improve search engine visibility and attract potential clients.

5. **Content Structuring:** Organized service information and project showcases to build trust and inform decision-making.

## Results

Delivered a professional, user-friendly website that reflects Inception Idea’s commitment to quality and client satisfaction.

Enhanced online presence, leading to increased inquiries and project opportunities.

Provided the company with a scalable platform for future updates and service additions.
