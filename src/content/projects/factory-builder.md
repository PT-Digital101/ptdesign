## Overview

Factory Builder is a Thai company specializing in turnkey industrial construction solutions, offering pre-designed and customizable buildings for factories, warehouses, stadiums, lifestyle centers, and business offices. Their approach emphasizes efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and timely delivery, aiming to simplify the construction process for clients across various industries.

## Objectives

1. Develop a user-friendly website that showcases Factory Builder’s diverse offerings and services.

2. Highlight key building types to demonstrate the company’s expertise and versatility.

3. Provide clear information about services offered, including standard designs, custom designs, and client-specific builds.

4. Enhance online visibility through SEO best practices and engaging content.

5. Ensure the website is mobile-responsive and easy to navigate.

## My Role (UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

As the UX/UI Designer and WordPress Developer, I led the project through several key phases:

1. **UX Strategy:** Designed an intuitive layout that guides users through services, building types, and contact information.

2. **Visual Design:** Created a clean, modern aesthetic that aligns with Factory Builder’s branding and values.

3. **WordPress Development:** Built a responsive website using WordPress, ensuring ease of content management and scalability.

4. **SEO Optimization:** Implemented SEO best practices to improve search engine visibility and attract potential clients.

5. **Content Structuring:** Organized service information and project showcases to build trust and inform decision-making.

## Results

Delivered a professional, user-friendly website that reflects Factory Builder’s commitment to quality and client satisfaction.

Enhanced online presence, leading to increased inquiries and project opportunities.

Provided the company with a scalable platform for future updates and service additions.
