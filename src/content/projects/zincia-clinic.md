## Overview

Zincia Aesthetic Clinic is a trusted beauty clinic in Ayutthaya, dedicated to providing ethical and personalized aesthetic treatments. With a focus on client safety and satisfaction, the clinic offers a range of services, including Ultraformer III, Filler Programs, Botox, Acne Treatments, Diode Laser Hair Removal, and various facial treatments. The clinic’s mission is to empower clients to achieve their best version through professional care and advanced technology.

## Objectives

1. Develop a user-friendly website that reflects the clinic’s commitment to quality and client care.

2. Present services clearly, emphasizing the benefits and safety of each treatment.

3. Enhance online presence to attract and inform potential clients.

4. Ensure the website is mobile-responsive and easy to navigate.

## My Role (UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

As the UX/UI Designer and WordPress Developer, I led the project through several key phases:

1. **UX Strategy:** Designed an intuitive layout that guides users through services, testimonials, and contact information.

2. **Visual Design:** Created a clean, modern aesthetic that aligns with the clinic’s branding and values.

3. **WordPress Development:** Built a responsive website using WordPress, ensuring ease of content management and scalability.

4. **Content Structuring:** Organized service information and client testimonials to build trust and inform decision-making.

## Results

Delivered a professional, user-friendly website that reflects Zincia Aesthetic Clinic’s commitment to quality and client care.

Enhanced online presence, leading to increased inquiries and appointments.

Provided the clinic with a scalable platform for future updates and service additions.
