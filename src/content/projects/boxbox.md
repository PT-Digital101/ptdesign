## Overview

BoxBox is a stylish online apparel store focused on **performance wear**—blending comfort, quality, and fashion with technical craftsmanship. With collections like "Sundae League" jerseys and fabric lines such as CloudSoft™, NexDry™, and LuxDry™, the website showcases a unique blend of lifestyle and innovation for fashion-conscious users seeking both style and functionality.

## Objectives

1. Present apparel products with clarity and appeal, aligning performance fabrics (CloudSoft™, NexDry™, LuxDry™) with lifestyle branding.

2. Simplify shopping with intuitive product discovery via categories and clear navigation.

3. Reinforce the brand’s values of “style and performance” through consistent, engaging visuals and messaging.

## My Role (UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

As the designer and developer, I guided the project through multiple key phases:

1. **UX Strategy:** Organized the site’s information architecture to guide users from fabric features to product categories (e.g., T-shirts, Polo, Sundae League).

2. **Visual & Interaction Design:** Created modern, brand-consistent mockups that highlight material performance and product benefits.

3. **WordPress Development:** Built a custom e-commerce platform using WooCommerce or a similar solution for easy content updates, product management, and secure checkout.

4. **Mobile Optimization:** Ensured a seamless shopping experience across devices, emphasizing touch interactions and responsive layouts.

## Results

Created a brand-centered, user-friendly shopping platform that draws attention to both fabric tech and product details.

Simplified navigation and checkout flow, resulting in a smoother user journey from discovery to purchase.

A flexible design system and WordPress backend ensure long-term scalability and ease of updates.
