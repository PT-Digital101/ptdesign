## Overview

Meikswe Myanmar is a non-profit organization dedicated to community development, education, and social empowerment in Myanmar. They needed a professional, accessible, and modern website to showcase their projects, engage with communities, and attract donors and partners worldwide.

## Objectives

Create a clean, user-friendly website that reflects the organization’s values and mission.

Make information about their projects, news, and resources easily accessible.

Build trust with international partners and donors through transparency and professional design.

Ensure the website is fully responsive and optimized for all devices.

## My Role (UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

As the UX/UI Designer and WordPress Web Developer, I led the full design and development process:

1. Conducted research into NGO websites to understand best practices for usability and credibility.

2. Designed a clear navigation structure with an emphasis on simplicity and accessibility.

3. Created engaging layouts for project updates and resource sections.

4. Integrated WordPress CMS for easy content management by the Meikswe Myanmar team.

5. Optimized SEO to improve visibility for local and global audiences.

## Results

Delivered a modern, professional website that strengthens Meikswe Myanmar’s digital presence.

Improved accessibility for both local communities and international donors.

Created a scalable platform that supports the organization’s long-term growth.
