## Overview

Berry Dent offers a unique hybrid toothpaste that combines the natural benefits of antioxidant-rich berries with proven fluoride protection. Their formula includes ingredients like raspberry, blueberry, blackberry, cranberry, and acai berry, aiming to provide a healthier, brighter, and more confident smile. The product is free from triclosan, parabens, and sugar, catering to health-conscious consumers seeking eco-friendly oral care solutions.

## Objectives

1. **E-commerce Platform:** Develop a user-friendly online store to facilitate product sales.

2. **Brand Identity:** Create a modern and appealing design that reflects the natural and innovative aspects of the product.

3. **Product Presentation:** Showcase the toothpaste’s unique ingredients and benefits effectively.

4. **SEO Optimization:** Implement strategies to enhance online visibility and attract potential customers.

## My Role (UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

As the UX/UI Designer and Developer, I led the project through several key phases:

1. **UX Strategy:** Designed an intuitive layout that guides users through product information, benefits, and purchasing options.

2. **Visual Design:** Developed a clean, modern aesthetic that aligns with Berry Dent’s branding and values.

3. **E-commerce Development:** Built a responsive online store using WooCommerce, ensuring ease of product management and secure transactions.

4. **SEO Optimization:** Implemented SEO best practices to improve search engine visibility and attract health-conscious consumers.

5. **Content Structuring:** Organized product information and benefits to inform decision-making and encourage purchases.

## Results

Delivered a professional, user-friendly website that reflects Berry Dent’s commitment to natural and effective oral care.

Enhanced online presence, leading to increased product awareness and sales.

Provided the company with a scalable platform for future product additions and updates.
