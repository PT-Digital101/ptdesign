## Overview

Rassapoom Clinic is a premium aesthetic and wellness clinic specializing in personalized beauty, facial contouring, skin treatments, cosmetic procedures, and holistic wellness services.

The project focused on transforming a large and complex service offering into a refined, easy-to-navigate digital experience that strengthens the clinic’s premium positioning while helping users understand treatments, discover the right services, build trust in the medical team, and move naturally toward consultation or appointment booking.

The website was designed as a scalable digital platform rather than simply a promotional clinic website, supporting a growing service catalog, multiple doctors, promotions, educational content, patient reviews, and five clinic locations.

## Objectives

1. Redesign the website to create a **modern, premium, and trustworthy digital presence** for Rassapoom Clinic.

2. Improve the **user experience and information architecture** across a large range of aesthetic treatments and services.

3. Make it easier for users to **discover treatments based on their needs and concerns**.

4. Strengthen trust through **doctor profiles, treatment information, reviews, results, and clinic expertise**.

5. Create clearer **conversion journeys** from treatment discovery to consultation, LINE contact, branch selection, and booking.

6. Deliver a fully **responsive experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile**.

7. Build a **scalable website structure** that supports future services, doctors, promotions, branches, and content.

## Results

Delivered a complete digital platform that better reflects Rassapoom Clinic’s premium medical and aesthetic positioning.

The redesigned information architecture makes a large treatment catalog easier to understand and navigate while establishing clearer relationships between services, doctors, reviews, educational content, and clinic locations.

The experience creates stronger trust signals throughout the customer journey and provides clearer paths from treatment discovery to consultation and appointment booking.

A reusable WordPress content system also gives the clinic a scalable foundation for expanding treatments, doctors, promotions, locations, and educational content while maintaining a consistent design language across the website.
