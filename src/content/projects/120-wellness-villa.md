## Overview

120 Wellness Villa Clinic is a modern health center dedicated to enhancing well-being through preventive medicine, anti-aging therapies, and personalized health programs. The clinic offers a range of services, including detoxification therapy, hormone balancing, weight loss solutions, IV nutrient therapy, and skin rejuvenation treatments. Their approach emphasizes holistic care, focusing on the root causes of health issues rather than merely alleviating symptoms.

## Objectives

1. Develop a user-friendly website that reflects the clinic’s commitment to personalized and preventive healthcare.

2. Clearly present the clinic’s services, treatment programs, and success stories to educate and attract potential clients.

3. Enhance online visibility through SEO best practices and engaging content.

4. Ensure the website is mobile-responsive and easy to navigate.

## My Role (UX/UI & WordPress Dev)

As the UX/UI Designer and WordPress Developer, I led the project through several key phases:

1. **UX Strategy:** Designed an intuitive layout that guides users through services, treatment programs, and success stories.

2. **Visual Design:** Created a clean, modern aesthetic that aligns with the clinic’s branding and values.

3. **WordPress Development:** Built a responsive website using WordPress, ensuring ease of content management and scalability.

4. **SEO Optimization:** Implemented SEO best practices to improve search engine visibility and attract local clients.

5. **Content Structuring:** Organized service information and client testimonials to build trust and inform decision-making.

## Results

Delivered a professional, user-friendly website that reflects 120 Wellness Villa Clinic’s commitment to personalized and preventive healthcare.

Enhanced online presence, leading to increased inquiries and appointments.

Provided the clinic with a scalable platform for future updates and service additions.
